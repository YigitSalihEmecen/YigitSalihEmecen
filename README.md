I build perception, simulation and teleoperation stacks for industrial mobile manipulators in **ROS 2**, and I tinker with embedded hardware, 3D printers and sound in my free time. I pair embedded-systems depth (Jetson, STM32, ESP32) with the planning discipline needed to deliver EU-funded research across institutions.

  <source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="assets/currently-dark.svg?v=4">

- **GIGABOT** at the TU Wien Institute of Production Engineering and Photonic Technologies (IFT): a Dockerized ROS 2 workspace on an NVIDIA Jetson Thor, drivers for the AMR and the arm, a Gazebo digital twin, and 5G teleoperation with a live 3D view.
- **AI.ROBOTECH**: coordinating a seven-partner Erasmus+ project on safe autonomous robotics education with ROS 2.
- **Master's** in Manufacturing & Robotics: machine vision, robot learning, robot control, mobile robotics.
- **Side quests**: a Game Boy emulator on an ESP32 with no PSRAM, and a €5 plotter made of 3D-printed parts.
